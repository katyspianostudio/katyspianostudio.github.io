About 关于

A pianist's path, from Beijing to Boston. 从北京到波士顿的音乐之路

⋄

Pianist · Educator · Boston 钢琴家 · 教育家 · 波士顿

Katy Chen is a concert pianist and piano educator based in Boston, Massachusetts. A graduate of the Central Conservatory of Music in Beijing and the New England Conservatory in Boston, where she earned her Master of Music in Piano Performance under the renowned Victor Rosenbaum, she is a Steinway Educational Partner and a member of several teachers' associations including MMTA (Massachusetts Music Teachers Association), NHMTA (New Hampshire Music Teachers Association), MTNA (Music Teachers National Association), and NEPTA (New England Piano Teachers Association). During her studies at the New England Conservatory, she was awarded an institutional scholarship and received multiple Dean's Awards in recognition of her academic and artistic achievement. 陈扬（Katy Chen），旅美青年钢琴演奏家、钢琴教育家，现居美国波士顿。她毕业于北京中央音乐学院与美国新英格兰音乐学院，并在新英格兰音乐学院师从国际著名钢琴家 Victor Rosenbaum 教授，获得钢琴演奏硕士学位。她现为 Steinway 教育合作伙伴（Steinway Educational Partner），同时也是多个专业教师协会的成员，包括美国音乐教师协会 MTNA（Music Teachers National Association）、麻省音乐教师协会 MMTA（Massachusetts Music Teachers Association）、新罕布什尔州音乐教师协会 NHMTA（New Hampshire Music Teachers Association），以及新英格兰钢琴教师协会 NEPTA（New England Piano Teachers Association）。在新英格兰音乐学院攻读硕士学位期间，她获得学院颁发的奖学金，并多次荣获院长奖（Dean's Award），以表彰其在学术与艺术上的卓越表现。

As a pianist she has received high praise from many master musicians, including Yin Chengzong, Liubov Rudova, Ilya Itin, Guangren Zhou, Jeffrey Cohen, Daniel Epstein, Benjamin Hochman, and Winston Choi. In 2005, she was selected to participate in a month-long master class with the celebrated Chinese pianist Yin Chengzong, where she performed works including Liszt's Rigoletto paraphrase and the Grieg Piano Concerto. 作为钢琴演奏家，她的演奏获得了众多国际钢琴大师的高度赞誉，包括殷承宗、Liubov Rudova、Ilya Itin、周广仁、Jeffrey Cohen、Daniel Epstein、Benjamin Hochman 以及 Winston Choi 等多位音乐界名家。2005 年，她获邀参加著名钢琴家殷承宗大师班为期一个月的专业学习，期间演奏了李斯特根据威尔第歌剧《弄臣》改编的钢琴改编曲，以及格里格钢琴协奏曲等作品。

Katy started learning to play the piano at the age of three and a half. At the age of nine, she was admitted to the Affiliated Elementary School of the Central Conservatory of Music in Beijing, beginning what would become more than a decade of professional training inside one of China's most selective musical institutions. She studied under many distinguished educators, including Professor Manchun Chen, Principal of the CCM Affiliated Elementary School, and Professor Zhiwei Zhang, doctoral supervisor at the Conservatory, who guided her through her affiliated middle school and high school years. In 2007, she was admitted to the Central Conservatory of Music for her undergraduate studies, where she received her Bachelor of Music in Piano Performance under the guidance of pianist Xiang Zou, the youngest gold medalist in the history of Canada's Honens International Piano Competition. In 2011, she was admitted to the New England Conservatory in Boston for graduate study, where she completed her Master of Music in 2013 under Professor Victor Rosenbaum. Since the beginning of her piano study, she has attended many piano performances and competitions, and received numerous praises and awards — including, as a teenager, a featured solo broadcast on Xiamen Television's Zheng Yue Zheng New Year Concert in 2004, where she performed Chopin's Scherzo No. 1 in B minor, Op. 20. Following her graduation, she settled in the Boston area and founded her own piano studio, offering lessons to students across all ages and levels. 陈扬三岁半开始学习钢琴。九岁时，她考入中央音乐学院附属小学，自此开启了她在中国专业音乐教育的最高学府长达十余年的专业训练生涯。在求学期间，她先后师从多位杰出的音乐教育家，包括中央音乐学院附小校长的陈曼春教授，以及中央音乐学院博士生导师张志玮教授。2007 年，她考入中央音乐学院本科，师从加拿大赫奈斯（Honens）国际钢琴大赛最年轻金奖得主——青年钢琴家邹翔教授，并获得钢琴演奏专业学士学位。2011 年，她赴美国波士顿的新英格兰音乐学院攻读研究生学位，并于 2013 年师从 Victor Rosenbaum 教授完成钢琴演奏硕士学位。自习琴以来，她多次参加各类钢琴演出与比赛，屡获嘉奖与赞誉。其中尤为令人难忘的是 2004 年，她作为青少年钢琴新秀受邀登上厦门市政府宣传部与厦门电视台联合主办的《正月正音乐会》，以肖邦《b 小调第一号谐谑曲》（作品 20 号）独奏开场，通过电视向全市观众现场直播。完成学业之后，她定居波士顿地区，创办了自己的私人钢琴工作室，为各个年龄段、各种程度的学生提供专业的钢琴教学。

Katy has performed extensively across the United States and China, including at Jordan Hall and Steinway Hall in Boston, Beijing Concert Hall, Xiamen University Auditorium, and Jimei University Concert Hall, as well as in recital across Cambridge, Boston, Beijing, and Xiamen. 陈扬的演奏足迹遍及中美两国，主要演出场地包括美国波士顿乔丹音乐厅（Jordan Hall）、美国波士顿斯坦威音乐厅（Steinway Hall）、北京音乐厅、厦门大学音乐厅以及集美大学音乐厅等。此外，她还在剑桥、波士顿、北京、厦门等多地举办过个人独奏音乐会与师生音乐会。

With more than twenty years of piano teaching experience, Katy has cultivated a generation of pianists who have gone on to make significant achievements under her supervision. In recent years, many students from her studio have won first-place prizes at the Young Artist Piano Competition, top prizes at the Bay State Piano Contest, and recognition at the San Francisco International Piano Competition and multiple adjudicated festivals across New England. Her students have also earned Distinction grades on ABRSM examinations at every level, and a number have gone on to study at leading music conservatories. Beyond competitions, Katy encourages her students to explore a wide range of musical genres — from classical and contemporary to popular repertoire — and to develop their own voice as musicians, not merely as performers. 陈扬深耕钢琴教育二十余载，培养了一批又一批优秀的钢琴学生，他们在她的悉心指导下取得了令人瞩目的成绩。近年来，她工作室的多名学生先后在 Young Artist Piano Competition 中荣获第一名，在 Bay State Piano Contest 中斩获优异名次，并入围 San Francisco International Piano Competition（旧金山国际钢琴比赛）以及新英格兰地区多届 Judged Festival，获得评委高度认可。她的学生在各个级别的 ABRSM 英皇钢琴考级中均取得 Distinction（优异）成绩，多名学生已进入国际知名音乐学院继续深造。除比赛之外，陈扬还鼓励学生广泛涉猎不同风格的音乐——从古典、当代到流行作品——引导他们成为真正具有独立音乐思考与个人艺术语言的音乐家，而不仅仅是演奏者。

At the heart of Katy's teaching is the conviction that music is a universal language requiring no translation — one that every student, regardless of age or background, can learn to speak in their own way. She is known for her patience, attentive listening, and ability to make each student feel valued, encouraged, and capable of reaching their highest potential. 陈扬的教学理念始终建立在一个核心信念之上：音乐是一门无需翻译的世界语言——无论年龄、无论背景，每一位学生都可以学会用自己的方式去诉说它。她以严谨的音乐素养、敏锐的音乐感知力，以及对学生发自内心的关怀，让每一位学生都感受到被尊重、被鼓励，并相信自己能够发挥最大潜能，赢得了业内同仁、学生与家长的高度认可。

Disciplined Musicianship 扎实的基本功

Conservatory-grade fundamentals — tone, technique, sight-reading, and listening — built patiently from the very first lesson. 从第一节课开始，循序渐进地建立音色、技巧、视奏与聆听的扎实基础。

♫

Personalized to Every Student 因材施教

Each student is met where they are. Lessons are tailored to age, goals, and learning style so every child or adult feels valued and heard. 每位学生都是独一无二的。课程内容因学生的年龄、目标与学习风格量身定制。

♬

Performance Confidence 舞台自信

Annual summer and Christmas recitals give students real performance experience — and a chance to share their progress with family. 每年夏季与圣诞汇报演出，让学生真正登台演奏，与家人分享学习成果。

★

Joy First 享受音乐

A warm, encouraging studio where students feel safe to take risks. The goal is a lifelong love of the piano, not just exam scores. 温暖鼓励的学习氛围，让学生敢于尝试、勇于表达。学琴的目标，是一生与音乐为伴。

Studio Policy 教学守则

Clear expectations, steady progress. 明确的期待，稳定的进步

A summary — full studio policy is shared upon enrollment. 以下为简要内容，完整教学守则将于入学时提供。

Lesson length课程时长
30, 45, or 60 minutes weekly — assigned by age and level.每周 30 / 45 / 60 分钟，根据年龄与程度安排。
Tuition学费
Monthly, due at the first lesson of the month. Cash or check.按月收取，每月第一节课时缴付，现金或支票。
Attendance出勤
Please give at least one week's notice for missed lessons (24 hours minimum). No make-up lessons.请假请提前一周告知（最迟 24 小时），原则上不安排补课。
Practice练习
At least 5 days per week — short daily sessions are far better than cramming.每周至少 5 天，每日短时练习远胜于临时突击。
Instrument at home家中乐器
Acoustic piano (tuned twice yearly) or a high-quality weighted digital piano — not a keyboard.需配备真钢琴（每年调律两次）或高品质重锤电钢琴（非普通电子琴）。
Recitals汇报演出
Studio recitals each summer and Christmas — pieces memorized, attendance expected.每年夏季与圣诞举办汇报演出，需背谱并出席。
